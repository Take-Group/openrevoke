# open-revoke/backend/app/catalog/logos/

Logos of the app catalog, served by GET `/api/catalog/logos/{slug}.png`. Made by `backend/scripts/fetch_catalog_logos.py`; procedure in `skills/backend/app-catalog.md`. Trademarks of their owners, not under the repo's MIT license.

- `*.png`: one 128x128 PNG per `slug` in `../apps.json` (transparent mark with a margin, or a full-bleed square).
